Mathematical Syntax Guide

How to enter equations in the graph plotter

Function Types & Examples

Linear
y = 2x + 1

Or -0.5x + 3, x - 4

Quadratic
y = x² or x^2 - 4

Supports superscripts like x² or ^2

Cubic
y = x³ or x^3 - 3x

Higher order polynomials like x^4 - 2x^2

Square Root
y = √x or sqrt(x)

Also sqrt(16 - x^2) or cbrt(x)

Exponential
y = 2^x or e^x

Use exp(x) for natural base e

Trigonometric
sin(x), cos(x), tan(x)

Angles in radians. Try sin(2x)

Absolute Value
|x| or abs(x)

E.g. |x^2 - 4| or abs(x) - 2

Logarithms & Constants
ln(x), log(x), pi, e

Constants: π or pi, Euler's e

Smart Parser Features

  • Implicit Multiplication: You can type 2x instead of 2*x, or 3(x+1), or x sin(x).
  • Prefixes: You can include y = or f(x) = or just write the expression directly like x^2.
  • Discontinuity Handling: Functions like tan(x) or 1/x will not draw false vertical connections across asymptotes!
